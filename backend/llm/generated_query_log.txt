Question:
Find customers who purchased at least 3 different products.

SQL:

Response:
SELECT Customer.CustomerID, Customer.Name
FROM Customer
JOIN SalesOrderHeader ON Customer.CustomerID = SalesOrderHeader.CustomerID
JOIN SalesOrderDetail ON SalesOrderHeader.SalesOrderID = SalesOrderDetail.SalesOrderID
GROUP BY Customer.CustomerID, Customer.Name
HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT SalesOrderDetail.ProductID)) >= 3;
