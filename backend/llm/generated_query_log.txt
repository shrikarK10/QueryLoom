Question:
Total revenue per product category

SQL:

Response:
```sql
SELECT 
    P.Name AS CategoryName,
    SUM(SLineTotal) AS RevenuePerCategory
FROM 
    Product P
JOIN 
    SalesOrderHeader SH ON P.ProductID = SH.ProductID
JOIN 
    SalesOrderDetail SDet ON SH.SalesOrderID = SDet.SalesOrderID
GROUP BY 
    P.Name, SDet.LineTotal
ORDER BY 
    RevenuePerCategory DESC;
```
